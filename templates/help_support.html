Frequently Asked Questions

What does the confidence score mean?

It's a 0–100 weighted blend of RSI momentum, volume surge, chart pattern strength, Fibonacci price position, trend alignment, and volatility. Scores above 75 are rare and worth a closer look — but always do your own research before trading.

How does the forecast "spaghetti model" work?

For any ticker, PipSqueak generates four probable 5-day price paths — momentum, retest, breakdown, and sideways — weighted by current volatility and trend. It's a scenario map, not a guarantee.

Why can I only track 5 stocks?

The tracked list powers voice alerts and focused pattern monitoring. Keeping it small keeps the signal-to-noise ratio high. Per-user watchlists with higher limits are on the post-beta roadmap.

Where does the market data come from?

Prices and volume come from free public feeds (Yahoo Finance via yfinance), so data can be delayed a few minutes. PipSqueak is an analysis tool, not a brokerage — never trade on delayed data alone.

Is PipSqueak financial advice?

No. PipSqueak surfaces technical setups and probabilities for educational purposes. Trading involves substantial risk of loss. Always do your own research and consider consulting a licensed financial advisor.
