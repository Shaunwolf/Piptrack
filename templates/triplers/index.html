Three times the price
in three weeks.

Every listed US stock that tripled within 15 trading days, lined up on the day before it launched. Each one is read on the 1-hour, 4-hour and daily charts (every Scan Lab block, Fibonacci, Gann and candlestick reading), then compared with ordinary windows from stocks at the same price, so a reading only counts if it shows up more before triplers.
